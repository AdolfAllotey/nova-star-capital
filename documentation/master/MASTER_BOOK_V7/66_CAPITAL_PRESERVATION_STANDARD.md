# Nova Star Capital
# Capital Preservation Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: Investment Governance

---

# Purpose

The first objective of Nova Star Capital is the preservation of capital.

Profitability is meaningful only if capital survives.

Every investment decision shall be evaluated against its potential impact on long-term capital preservation.

---

# Primary Objective

Protect capital.

Secondary Objective

Compound capital.

---

# Guiding Principles

Never expose the platform to catastrophic risk.

Avoid irreversible losses.

Prefer sustainable growth over rapid growth.

Accept missed opportunities.

Reject unacceptable risks.

Cash is a strategic asset.

---

# Capital Hierarchy

1. Preserve capital

2. Preserve liquidity

3. Preserve optionality

4. Generate returns

---

# Risk Acceptance

Risk is accepted only when:

Expected return justifies exposure.

Maximum downside is understood.

Risk limits remain respected.

Portfolio diversification remains acceptable.

Liquidity remains sufficient.

---

# Capital Allocation

Capital allocation shall always consider:

Current market regime

Portfolio exposure

Asset correlations

Liquidity

Volatility

Risk concentration

---

# Drawdown Philosophy

Temporary drawdowns are expected.

Permanent capital impairment is unacceptable.

Recovery capability shall always be preserved.

---

# Cash Management

Cash shall not be viewed as idle capital.

Cash provides:

Flexibility

Opportunity

Protection

Optionality

---

# Diversification

Diversification shall reduce portfolio fragility.

Diversification shall not become diworsification.

Every allocation must have a justified purpose.

---

# Risk Limits

Risk limits exist to protect capital.

Risk limits shall never be bypassed for performance reasons.

---

# Portfolio Scaling

Capital growth shall increase prudently.

Position sizing shall evolve gradually.

Aggressive scaling is prohibited.

---

# Loss Management

Losses shall be:

Expected

Measured

Documented

Reviewed

Used for improvement

Never ignored

---

# Investment Discipline

No investment shall threaten the long-term survival of the platform.

---

# Final Principle

Capital preserved today creates opportunities tomorrow.
