# Nova Star Capital
# Technology Evolution Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: Architecture Governance

---

# Purpose

Technology continuously evolves.

Nova Star Capital shall continuously evaluate technological innovations while preserving platform stability, security and operational excellence.

Innovation shall be disciplined.

---

# Objectives

Support innovation

Reduce technological risk

Maintain architectural consistency

Avoid unnecessary complexity

Protect production stability

---

# Guiding Principles

Technology shall solve a real problem.

Technology shall create measurable value.

Technology shall not increase operational risk.

Technology shall remain maintainable.

Technology shall remain observable.

# Proof of Concept

Every significant technology shall first be evaluated through an isolated Proof of Concept.

The objective is learning rather than deployment.

---

# Architecture Compatibility

Every new technology shall be compatible with:

Modular Architecture

Decision Hierarchy

Data Governance

Model Governance

Security Standards

Monitoring Standards

Operational Standards

---

# Artificial Intelligence

AI technologies shall be evaluated for:

Explainability

Reliability

Operational usefulness

Bias

Security

Operational cost

Governance compatibility

---

# Vendor Evaluation

External providers shall be evaluated according to:

Financial stability

Security posture

Operational maturity

Support quality

API stability

Documentation

Long-term viability

---

# Production Requirements

No technology shall enter production without:

Architecture validation

Operational validation

Security validation

Performance validation

Documentation

Rollback capability

---

# Retirement

Technologies shall be retired when:

No longer maintained

Security risks emerge

Operational costs become excessive

Superior alternatives exist

Architecture changes require replacement

---

# Continuous Review

Technology reviews shall be performed periodically.

Innovation is encouraged.

Uncontrolled innovation is prohibited.

---

# Final Principle

Technology serves the platform.

The platform shall never become dependent on technology alone.
