# Nova Star Capital
# Decision Lifecycle Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: Core Decision Framework

---

# Purpose

This document defines the complete lifecycle of every trading decision inside Nova Star Capital.

A trading decision does not stop after an entry.

Every position follows a controlled lifecycle until archival.

---

---

# Stage 1 — Opportunity Detection

Market produces potential opportunities.

No decision exists yet.

The objective is discovery only.

Possible sources:

Momentum

Breakout

Whales

Macro

News

Funding

Market regime

AI detection

---

# Stage 2 — Validation

The candidate is validated.

Checks include:

Liquidity

Confidence

Correlation

Risk

Market regime

Portfolio compatibility

Capital availability

Rejected candidates are archived as rejected.

---

# Stage 3 — Decision Creation

A Decision Object is created.

Decision ID

Creation time

Strategy

Confidence

Risk

Expected holding period

Target allocation

Expected outcome

Initial assumptions

---

# Stage 4 — Approval

Portfolio Brain

Risk Engine

Capital Allocator

Execution Planner

must all approve.

One veto blocks execution.

---

# Stage 5 — Execution

Execution creates:

Position ID

Execution ID

Broker ID

Fill price

Fees

Slippage

Timestamp

---

# Stage 6 — Active Monitoring

The decision remains alive.

Monitoring includes:

PnL

Volatility

Market regime

Portfolio exposure

Correlation

Drawdown

Liquidity

News

Whales

Macro

---

# Stage 7 — Decision Evolution

A decision may evolve.

Possible actions:

Maintain

Increase

Reduce

Partial TP

Move stop

Trailing stop

Freeze

Risk reduction

Temporary suspension

No action

Every change creates a new decision event.

---

# Stage 8 — Exit

Exit reasons include:

Take Profit

Partial TP

Trailing Stop

Stop Loss

Risk reduction

Portfolio rebalance

Market regime change

Emergency exit

Manual intervention

Broker failure

---

# Stage 9 — Post Analysis

Every completed decision is analysed.

Metrics:

Return

Duration

Risk

Execution quality

Slippage

Strategy quality

Market conditions

Opportunity cost

---

# Stage 10 — Learning

Learning engine updates:

Strategy statistics

Confidence calibration

Risk calibration

Expected duration

Expected volatility

Broker quality

Execution quality

---

# Stage 11 — Archival

Completed decisions become immutable.

Archived information remains available for:

Audit

Dashboard

Analytics

AI learning

Backtesting

Compliance

Reporting

---

# Explainability

At any time the platform must explain:

Why entered?

Why maintained?

Why increased?

Why reduced?

Why exited?

Why this broker?

Why this allocation?

Why now?

---

# Final Principle

A position is not a trade.

A position is the visible consequence of a living decision that continuously evolves until completion.
