# Nova Star Capital
# Regulatory Readiness Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: Governance

---

# Purpose

Nova Star Capital is designed to remain adaptable to future regulatory requirements.

Although the platform currently operates as a proprietary trading company, its architecture shall support future regulatory evolution without major redesign.

Compliance readiness is an architectural principle.

---

# Objectives

Maintain transparent operations

Support future regulatory obligations

Facilitate external audits

Maintain complete traceability

Protect confidential information

Support institutional partnerships

# Audit Readiness

The platform shall be capable of demonstrating:

Trading decisions

Portfolio evolution

Risk decisions

Execution history

Configuration changes

System changes

Model versions

User actions

Incident history

---

# Record Retention

Historical information shall remain recoverable.

Examples:

Trades

Orders

Portfolio snapshots

Daily reports

Risk reports

Dashboard exports

Configuration history

Audit logs

---

# Traceability

Every important decision shall be traceable.

Traceability includes:

Source

Timestamp

Decision Engine

Input Data

Output

Version

Operator (if applicable)

---

# Internal Controls

Critical operations shall include appropriate controls.

Examples:

Configuration approval

Deployment approval

Production validation

Risk validation

Emergency procedures

---

# Reporting

The platform shall support the generation of:

Daily reports

Weekly reports

Monthly reports

Annual summaries

Performance reports

Risk reports

Audit reports

---

# Security Readiness

Security documentation shall remain current.

Critical assets shall remain identifiable.

Administrative access shall be controlled.

---

# External Review

The platform shall remain capable of supporting:

Technical audits

Operational audits

Security assessments

Architecture reviews

Governance reviews

---

# Continuous Adaptation

New regulatory requirements shall be evaluated periodically.

The architecture shall evolve without compromising operational stability.

Compliance is easier when architecture anticipates future requirements.
