# Nova Star Capital
## Crypto Decision Engine

Version: V7
Status: Official
Classification: Core Strategy

---

# Mission

The Crypto Engine is the primary alpha generation engine of Nova Star Capital.

Its objective is not to trade continuously.

Its objective is to deploy capital only when high-quality opportunities exist.

---

# Investment Universe

The engine focuses on:

- large-cap cryptocurrencies
- mid-cap cryptocurrencies
- emerging narratives
- high-quality momentum
- institutional accumulation
- exceptional breakouts

Micro-cap speculation is excluded.

---

# Sources of Intelligence

The Crypto Engine combines multiple independent information sources.

Examples include:

Market momentum

Trend persistence

Volume acceleration

Relative strength

Volatility

Whale activity

Social momentum

Exchange flows

Market discovery

Cross-exchange confirmation

---

# Discovery Layer

The Discovery Engine continuously searches for opportunities.

Sources include:

Binance

MEXC

Bitpanda

CoinGecko

CoinMarketCap

Future sources may be added without changing the Portfolio Brain.

---

# Candidate Evaluation

Each candidate receives multiple independent scores.

Examples:

Momentum Score

Discovery Score

Volume Score

Persistence Score

Social Score

Risk Score

Liquidity Score

Institutional Score

No single score is sufficient.

---

# Signal Validation

Signals require confirmation.

Typical validations include:

minimum liquidity

minimum persistence

acceptable volatility

acceptable spread

risk limits

market regime compatibility

---

# Position Sizing

Position sizing is determined by the Capital Brain.

Inputs include:

portfolio exposure

available cash

risk budget

current regime

existing positions

confidence

The Crypto Engine never allocates capital directly.

---

# Portfolio Interaction

The Crypto Engine proposes.

The Portfolio Brain decides.

Risk constrains.

Governance validates.

Execution executes.

---

# Exit Logic

Positions may be closed because of:

target reached

risk reduction

portfolio concentration
