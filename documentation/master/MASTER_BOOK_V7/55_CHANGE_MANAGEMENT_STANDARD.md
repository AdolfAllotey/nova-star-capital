Version: V1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the official Change Management process for Nova Star Capital.

Every modification affecting the platform shall follow a controlled lifecycle.

No production change shall occur without validation.

---

# Core Principle

Every change is a controlled event.

No direct production modification is permitted.
