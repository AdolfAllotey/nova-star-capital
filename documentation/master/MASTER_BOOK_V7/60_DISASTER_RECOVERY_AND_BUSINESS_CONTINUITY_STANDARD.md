# Nova Star Capital
# Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: Governance

# Purpose

Nova Star Capital shall remain operational under adverse conditions.

Business Continuity ensures critical services continue operating.

Disaster Recovery ensures rapid restoration after major failures.

---

# Objectives

Protect capital

Protect data

Protect operations

Protect investors

Protect infrastructure

Minimize downtime

Minimize financial impact

Guarantee recoverability

---

# Definitions

Business Continuity (BCP)

Maintain critical operations during disruption.

Disaster Recovery (DRP)

Restore systems after disruption.

# Recovery Objectives

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Critical services restored as quickly as operationally feasible.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Historical trading records shall not be lost.

Portfolio state shall remain recoverable.
