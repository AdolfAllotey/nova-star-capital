DECISION TRACEABILITY
==============================================================================

Every decision must be reproducible.

For every order NSC must be able to explain:

Why was the asset discovered?

Why did it receive this score?

Why was it validated?

Why was it sized this way?

Why was capital available?

Why was governance approved?

Why was execution allowed?

Why wasn't another asset selected?

No decision may remain unexplained.
