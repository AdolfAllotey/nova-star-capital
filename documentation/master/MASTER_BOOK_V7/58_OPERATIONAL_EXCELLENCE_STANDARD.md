# Nova Star Capital
# Operational Excellence Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: Governance

---

# Objective

Operational Excellence ensures that every component of Nova Star Capital operates with institutional-grade reliability, consistency and quality.

Performance is measured not only by profitability but also by operational discipline.

---

# Core Principles

Reliability

Availability

Consistency

Repeatability

Automation

Continuous Improvement

---

# Operational Objectives

The platform shall:

produce identical outputs from identical inputs

avoid manual interventions whenever possible

recover automatically from failures

detect anomalies early

maintain complete traceability

remain observable at all times
