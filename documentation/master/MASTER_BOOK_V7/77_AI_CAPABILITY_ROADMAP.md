# Purpose

Artificial Intelligence is a strategic capability of Nova Star Capital.

Its objective is to augment human expertise and improve operational efficiency while preserving institutional governance.

AI shall assist.

AI shall never replace governance.

---

# Vision

Nova Star Capital aims to become an AI-Augmented Investment Platform.

Artificial Intelligence enhances:

Analysis

Research

Reporting

Monitoring

Simulation

Decision Support

Operational Excellence

without replacing human accountability.
