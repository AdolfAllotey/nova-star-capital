# Production Readiness Scorecard

Version: V1.0
Status: Executive
Classification: Production Governance

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official Production Readiness assessment used before any major deployment.

Every production release shall be evaluated against this scorecard.

Production approval is based on objective readiness criteria rather than subjective judgment.

---

# Readiness Levels

READY

The capability satisfies all mandatory requirements.

Deployment may proceed.

---

READY WITH CONDITIONS

Minor issues remain.

Deployment is permitted with documented mitigation plans.

---

NOT READY

Critical requirements are missing.

Deployment shall not proceed.
