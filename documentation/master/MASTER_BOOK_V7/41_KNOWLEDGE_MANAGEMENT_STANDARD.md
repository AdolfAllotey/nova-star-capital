# Nova Star Capital
# Knowledge Management Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: Operations

---

# Purpose

Knowledge is considered a strategic asset of Nova Star Capital.

This standard defines how operational, technical, financial and architectural knowledge is captured, validated, maintained and shared throughout the platform lifecycle.

The objective is to eliminate knowledge silos and ensure long-term maintainability.

---

# 1. Objectives

Knowledge Management shall ensure:

Knowledge preservation

Operational continuity

Faster onboarding

Reduced operational risk

Consistent decision making

Continuous learning

---

# 4. Documentation Principles

Every document shall be:

Version controlled

Reviewed

Traceable

Consistent

Searchable

Continuously updated

No undocumented production behaviour is acceptable.

---

# 5. Decision Records

Major architectural decisions should generate an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

Each ADR contains:

Context

Problem

Alternatives

Decision

Consequences

Review history

---

# 6. Lessons Learned

Every significant event should generate lessons learned.

Examples:

Trading failures

Production incidents

Unexpected market behaviour

Broker outages

Security events

Deployment issues

---

# 7. Knowledge Validation

Knowledge shall be periodically reviewed to verify:

Accuracy

Relevance

Completeness

Consistency

Obsolete information shall be archived.
