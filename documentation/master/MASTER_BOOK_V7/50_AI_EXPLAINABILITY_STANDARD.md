# Nova Star Capital
# AI Explainability Standard

Version: V1.0
Status: Official Documentation
Classification: AI / Explainability

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Explainability Layer of Nova Star Capital.

Every investment decision shall be fully explainable.

The platform must always be able to explain:

what happened

why it happened

why alternatives were rejected

which engines participated

which risks were identified

what may happen next

---

# Core Principle

No black-box decisions are allowed.

Every investment action must remain understandable by humans.

Explainability is mandatory.

---

# Explainability Scope

The Explainability Layer covers:

Discovery

Validation

Signal Generation

Portfolio Brain

Risk Engine

Capital Allocation

Execution Planning

Broker Selection

Execution

Monitoring

Exit

Learning

---

# Decision Questions

For every decision the platform shall answer:

Why this asset?

Why now?

Why this strategy?

Why this allocation?

Why this broker?

Why this execution plan?

Why this confidence?

Why this stop loss?

Why this take profit?

Why this holding duration?

---

# Rejected Alternatives

The platform stores:

Rejected assets

Rejected brokers

Rejected strategies

Rejected allocations

Rejected execution plans

Rejected risk profiles

Each rejection includes the reason.

# Risk Explanation

The platform explains:

# Portfolio Brain Explanation

Portfolio Brain shall explain:

Why the portfolio accepted the position

Why another position has priority

Why capital was limited

Why exposure was reduced

Why diversification required changes

Fallback usage

# Exit Explanation

Exit shall explain:

Take Profit reached

Stop Loss reached

Trailing Stop

Risk reduction

Portfolio rebalance

Market regime change

Emergency exit

Manual override

---

# Human Readable Summary

Every decision produces a natural language explanation.

Example:

"The position was opened because momentum, liquidity and market regime aligned positively while portfolio exposure remained below target. Risk Engine approved the sizing and Broker Router selected the venue with the best expected execution quality."
