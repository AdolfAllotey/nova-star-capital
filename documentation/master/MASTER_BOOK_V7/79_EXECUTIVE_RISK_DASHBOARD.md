# Executive Risk Dashboard

Version: V1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the Executive Risk Dashboard used to monitor the overall health of Nova Star Capital.

Its objective is to provide immediate visibility into critical risks and define the corresponding governance actions.

---

# Executive Dashboard Philosophy

Executives shall focus on:

Critical indicators

Emerging risks

Portfolio stability

Platform health

Operational resilience

Governance compliance

---

# Risk Classification

Green

Normal operating conditions.

No action required.

---

Amber

Increased attention required.

Operational review initiated.

Monitoring frequency increased.

---

Red

Critical condition.

Immediate escalation required.

Protective actions may be activated.
